# Code of Conduct

This is a small open-source project. The expectations are simple:

- **Be respectful.** Treat other contributors the way you would want to be treated.
- **Be constructive.** Critique ideas and code, not people. When you disagree, explain why and propose an alternative.
- **Stay on topic.** Issues and PRs are for the project. Take broader conversations elsewhere.
- **Assume good faith.** Most misunderstandings come from missing context, not malice.

Behavior that makes the project a worse place to contribute — harassment, personal attacks, sustained disruption — is not welcome. The maintainer will remove comments, close issues, or block contributors as needed to keep things workable.

Report concerns privately to **gary@crunchy.tools**.
